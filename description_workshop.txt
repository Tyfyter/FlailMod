This mod aims to rework the flail subclass, turning into a viable and fun challenge and fully utilizing all of its mechanics.
It adds a charge bar to flail attacks, adding up to 200% damage to fully charged hits, and accessories to go with it.
The Flairon, Anchor, Blue Moon and Drippler Crippler have also been reworked to use the charge bar.

It appears that unlike my spear rework, mod compatibility for this one has to be done on a case by case basis.
The mod currently features Thorium Mod compatibility. I will look into finding a solution for this, and if it fails eventually add other popular mods manually and/or mod calls in the future.
